Privacy Policy

App: PDF Reader – PDF Viewer (com.aianthro.pdftools)
Developer: AIAnthro
Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Our Privacy Promise: PDF Reader – PDF Viewer is an on-device utility app designed with zero cloud upload architecture. We never collect, upload, transmit, or share your documents, PDF files, scanned images, or personal signatures.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • PDF Documents & Files: All document viewing, merging, splitting, compressing, watermarking, text extraction, and signing happen 100% locally on your device. No files are ever sent to our servers.
  • Personal Signatures: Digital signatures drawn in the app are applied locally and stored solely within the resulting PDF saved on your device storage.
  • Passwords: Document passwords entered to view or unlock protected PDFs are processed strictly in-memory and are never transmitted over the internet or saved remotely.

2. Storage Permissions & Scoped Storage

Our app strictly follows Google Play Store Scoped Storage policies and Android storage guidelines:

  • The app uses Android standard storage pickers and MediaStore APIs to read PDFs you choose and to save exported files to public directories (Downloads/PDFReader/ and Pictures/PDFReader/).
  • We do not require or request broad device storage permissions (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We provide this app completely free of charge. To support development, the app displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob.

  • Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers such as your device's Advertising ID, approximate location (IP-based), and app interaction diagnostics solely to serve, personalize, and measure ad performance.
  • For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
